Found an anatomical error?
We take accuracy seriously. If you spot something that looks wrong — a bone in the wrong place, an incorrect adjacency, a mislabelled structure — please let us know. Include the game, the bone name, and what you believe is incorrect and we'll review it promptly.

Want to suggest a feature?
Anatomle is actively being developed. We're always interested in hearing what anatomy learners actually want — new game modes, new regions, better explanations. Drop us a line.
