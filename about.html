About

Learn anatomy
by playing

Anatomle is a daily anatomy puzzle. You get two structures in the human body, and your job is to name everything that connects them, one guess at a time, on a 3D model that lights up as you go.

What is Anatomle?

Each puzzle gives you a start and an end, like the clavicle and the radius. You type the bones (or muscles) that link them. A guess on the shortest route turns green, a guess that works but takes the long way turns yellow, and a guess that doesn't connect at all turns red.

There is one shared daily puzzle that resets at midnight UTC and builds your streak, plus unlimited practice games whenever you want more.

The games

Osteomle is all about bones. Play the full skeleton, or warm up on the skull, the hand, or the foot.

Myomle moves on to the 58 muscles of the upper limb. In Standard mode muscles connect through the bones they attach to. In the Nerve Challenge they connect through the nerves that supply them.

Who is it for?

Students revising for anatomy exams, healthcare and sports science learners, artists, and anyone curious about how the body fits together. Every puzzle takes a few minutes, and seeing how structures actually connect sticks far better than memorising a list.

Educational disclaimer: Anatomle is intended for educational entertainment only. It is not a clinical reference and should not be used as a primary source for medical or anatomical study. While every effort is made to ensure accuracy, errors may be present. Always consult qualified educational resources and licensed professionals.
